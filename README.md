## 📚 Overview

Electrical Engineering – Electro Midset is a modern educational platform designed to provide students, technicians, and engineering enthusiasts with access to Electrical and Electronics Engineering learning resources.

The platform serves as a centralized hub where learners can explore engineering topics, access study materials, strengthen technical knowledge, and support their academic or professional development journey.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Provide easy access to engineering learning materials
- Support Electrical & Electronics Engineering students
- Create a structured knowledge-sharing platform
- Promote self-learning and continuous professional growth
- Make engineering resources accessible from any device
