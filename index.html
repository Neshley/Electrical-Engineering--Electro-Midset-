📖 Verified Electrical Engineering Formula Library

Searchable repository of foundational electrical, power systems, machine, and electronic formulas with SI units and definitions.

Ohm's Law
V = I × R  |  I = V / R  |  R = V / I
Relates electric potential difference (V), current (I), and resistance (R) in a linear conductor.
V: Volts (V) • I: Amperes (A) • R: Ohms (Ω)
Electrical Power (Joule's Law)
P = V × I = I² × R = V² / R
Rate at which electrical energy is transferred by an electric circuit per unit time.
P: Watts (W) • V: Volts (V) • I: Amperes (A) • R: Ohms (Ω)
Three-Phase Active Power
P = √3 × VL × IL × cos(φ)
Real power consumed by a balanced three-phase AC load across line parameters.
VL: Line Voltage (V) • IL: Line Current (A) • cos(φ): Power factor
Synchronous Speed of AC Motor
Ns = (120 × f) / P
Rotational speed of stator's rotating magnetic field in an AC induction machine.
Ns: RPM • f: Frequency (Hz) • P: Number of poles
Motor Shaft Torque
T = (9550 × P_kW) / N_rpm
Mechanical twisting torque output produced at the rotating motor shaft.
T: Newton-meters (Nm) • P_kW: Output power (kW) • N: RPM
Transformer Turns Ratio & Currents
V1 / V2 = N1 / N2 = I2 / I1 = a
Voltage and current transformation ratios based on primary and secondary windings.
V: Volts (V) • N: Turns • I: Current (A) • a: Ratio
Transformer Short Circuit Fault
Isc = I_FLA / (%Z / 100)
Prospective symmetrical root-mean-square fault current on the transformer secondary.
Isc: Fault Current (A) • %Z: Percent Impedance
Cable Voltage Drop (1-Phase)
ΔV = 2 × I × L × (ρ / A)
Voltage drop along a two-core single-phase conductor run back and forth.
ΔV: Volts (V) • L: Length (m) • A: Area (mm²) • ρ: Resistivity
Capacitive Reactance
XC = 1 / (2π × f × C)
Opposition offered to the flow of alternating current by pure capacitance.
XC: Ohms (Ω) • f: Frequency (Hz) • C: Farads (F)
Inductive Reactance
XL = 2π × f × L
Opposition offered to the flow of alternating current by pure inductance.
XL: Ohms (Ω) • f: Frequency (Hz) • L: Henries (H)
RLC Series Resonant Frequency
f0 = 1 / [ 2π × √(L × C) ]
Frequency where inductive and capacitive reactances cancel out, leaving Z = R minimum.
f0: Hertz (Hz) • L: Henries (H) • C: Farads (F)
Power Factor Correction Capacitor
Qc = P × [ tan(φ1) - tan(φ2) ]
Reactive power needed to raise operating power factor from cos(φ1) to cos(φ2).
Qc: kVAR • P: Active kW • φ: Phase angles
